ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Andrew L. Yeager Jr.

Owner • Writer • Director • Producer

Born and raised in Detroit, Michigan, Andrew L. Yeager Jr. is the founder and owner of AYMann Productions.

Andrew discovered his passion for writing at the age of 16. As his journey continued, he came to recognize that writing, producing, and directing were more than creative interests—they were God-given gifts. What began as a personal outlet and source of healing soon became something much greater: a way to inspire, encourage, and bring healing to others through the power of storytelling—not just to those who come to witness, but even to those who are apart.

In 2016, Andrew brought his first live theatrical production to the stage, serving as its writer, director, and producer. That experience helped establish the foundation for what AYMann Productions represents today.

AYMann Productions is not simply about connecting with people who already have influence, experience, or a platform. A major part of its purpose is creating opportunities for people who may have never been given one—those who may have been overlooked or who believe it is too late to pursue the gifts inside of them.

OUR MISSION

The mission of AYMann Productions is to spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ through theatrical performances and powerful storytelling.

Andrew seeks to reach those who may feel lost, broken, discouraged, or disconnected from their faith and lead them toward—or back to—Jesus Christ.

Through inspirational theatrical productions, our stories acknowledge the reality of life’s struggles while shining an even greater light on hope, faith, restoration, and the truth that the troubles we face will never outshine the God we serve.
