AYMann Productions was founded by Detroit writer, director, and producer Andrew L. Yeager Jr. to bring honest, inspirational stories to the stage.

What began as a way to heal became a way to connect. Since bringing his first live production to the stage in 2016, Andrew has built AYMann around a simple belief: the stories we tell can help people find their way back to hope.
